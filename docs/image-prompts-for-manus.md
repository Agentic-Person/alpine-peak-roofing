## TIPS FOR MANUS / MIDJOURNEY

1. **Always add** `--style raw --v 6` for most realistic photography
2. **For consistency** across a series, add `--seed [number]` and use the same seed for related shots
3. **Lighting**: "golden hour" produces the most premium and warm results for roofing photography
4. **Avoid**: AI-generated looking images by keeping prompts very specific and realistic
5. **Size matters**: For website hero images, always use `--ar 16:9`. For portrait/team: `--ar 3:4`
6. **Before images** should explicitly mention: weathered, aged, missing granules, moss — to look authentically damaged
7. **After images** should match exact same camera angle, time of day, and weather as the Before version
